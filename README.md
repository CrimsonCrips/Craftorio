Craftorio(ALPHA) is a mod that has you use points to expand your reach of your world! Expand through chunks or a world border like border, Earn points done through sinking items through a Sinker,incentivizing players to mass produce items in their own ways and reap those points! Accept contracts and find items to gain buffs to make number go up!

Collaborate or Compete with other players, For The Points Shall Flow!

For Modpackers, contracts, effects and item values are data-driven,allowing for customizability and the ability to add their own for their desires and challenges!

Repo containing an example mod and a wiki to guide how to use it: https://github.com/CrimsonCrips/CraftorioTemplate
