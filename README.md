Craftorio(ALPHA) is a mod that has you use points to expand your reach of your world
Earning points is done through sinking items through a Sinker
This allows players to mass produce items like a factory through the use of farms and if installed, mods like Create, AE2, etc etc
The value of items is changeable freely through the use of datapacks, including the ability to assign values from other mods as well!
